Agricultural extension training

Crop Season

Water keeps a crop alive. Food makes it grow. Too much of either kills it. See a field at real size on real ground, and keep it going for a season.

Step 1

Scan a seed packet

Point your camera at a printed seed packet. The crop grows out of the card, so you can see how much room one plant really needs.

Checking your device Step 2

Grow a season

Put a planting bed on the floor in front of you. Water it, feed it, and harvest whatever survives five weeks.

Checking your device

Before you scan

Marker mode looks for one printed card. Open it, print it on paper, then point the camera at it. Holding a phone or a monitor up to the camera adds glare and tracks worse.
