## 3D models

All five were downloaded from [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza).

| File | Original | Author | Licence | Changes made |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `models/crop-a.glb` | Grass, used here as Paddy | Quaternius | CC0 | Texture resized 1024 to 512 and converted PNG to WebP. Mesh dedup, weld, join, simplify |
| `models/crop-b.glb` | Plant Big, used here as Pineapple | Quaternius | CC0 | Same pipeline |
| `models/fence.glb` | Fence | Quaternius | CC0 | None. 188 triangles, no textures |
| `models/bag.glb` | Bag Open, used here as the fertiliser sack | Quaternius | CC0 | None. 280 triangles, no textures |
| `models/can.glb` | Watering can | **Poly by Google** | **CC BY 4.0** | Normals stripped, welded, simplified, pruned. 2056 triangles down to 718, and 125 168 bytes down to 10 136 |

**Four of these are CC0, which is public domain and carries no attribution requirement. The
watering can is not.** It is CC BY 4.0, so crediting Poly by Google is a condition of the licence.

The unmodified originals are kept outside this folder so the before and after figures above stay
checkable.

A second fence, `fence_simpleLow` from the Kenney Nature Kit, was downloaded and tried early on.
At 19.5 cm it read as a rim rather than a fence on a 40 cm bed, and it came from a different
artist to the crops, so the scene looked assembled from parts. It was dropped. Nothing from that
pack is in this build.

## Libraries

| Library | Version | Used by | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A-Frame](https://aframe.io) | 1.5.0 | `marker.html`, loaded from its CDN | MIT |
| [MindAR](https://github.com/hiukim/mind-ar-js) | 1.2.5 | `marker.html`, loaded from its CDN | MIT |
| [A-Frame](https://aframe.io) | 1.8.0 | `markerless.html`, served from `vendor/` | MIT |

`vendor/aframe-1.8.0.min.js` is unmodified. It is byte for byte what
`https://aframe.io/releases/1.8.0/aframe.min.js` served on 4 September 2026, 1 323 396 bytes.

There are two reasons for the local copy, recorded here because a vendored library is easy to
mistake for own work. Markerless mode is meant to run with no internet, which cannot be true
while the library itself is a network fetch. And a version pinned on somebody else's server is
not really pinned.

## Fonts

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Roboto msdf atlas, `vendor/aframe-cdn/fonts/Roboto-msdf.json` and `.png` | Distributed with A-Frame, from `cdn.aframe.io` | Roboto is Apache 2.0, the atlas is MIT with A-Frame |

This is A-Frame's default typeface for anything drawn with its `text` component, which here is
the two tool labels. A-Frame normally fetches it from its CDN at runtime, silently. It is served
from this repository instead, with `window.AFRAME_CDN_ROOT` pointed at the local folder.
Unmodified.
